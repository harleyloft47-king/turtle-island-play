# Turtle Island

A free collectible card game of Indigenous history in Canada: 112 cards in 8 series, with real museum pictures that are public domain (CC0).

**Play:** https://harleyloft47-king.github.io/turtle-island-play/

Rip open packs, answer each card's question to unlock it, earn points to buy more packs, and complete each series. Works on phones and computers. Progress is saved in your own browser.

Picture credits: The Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Cleveland Museum of Art (open access). Full credits are listed in the game.

Legends are told differently by each Nation and storyteller. Teachers are encouraged to invite a local Knowledge Keeper to share more.
